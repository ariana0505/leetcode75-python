# 49. Longest Substring Without Repeating Characters / Subcadena Mas Larga sin Caracteres Repetidos

## English

Given a string `s`, find the **length of the longest substring** without duplicate characters.

### Examples

#### Example 1
```text
Input: s = "abcabcbb"
Output: 3
Explanation: The answer is "abc", with a length of 3. Note that "bca" and "cab" are also valid answers.
```

#### Example 2
```text
Input: s = "bbbbb"
Output: 1
Explanation: The answer is "b", with a length of 1.
```

#### Example 3
```text
Input: s = "pwwkew"
Output: 3
Explanation: The answer is "wke", with a length of 3. Notice that the answer must be a substring; "pwke" is a subsequence, not a substring.
```

### Constraints

- `0 <= s.length <= 5 * 10^4`
- `s` consists of English letters, digits, symbols, and spaces.
